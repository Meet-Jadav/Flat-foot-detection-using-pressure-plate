DATASET INFORMATION
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Training Data: 4,739 foot samples
  • Source: OHM 3000 pressure plate
  • Extracted from ~1,400 CSV files
  • Subjects: 1-3 (limited, but enough for POC)
  • Conditions: normal, antalgic, lurching

Class Distribution:
  • Normal: 926 (19.5%)
  • Flatfoot: 3,813 (80.5%)
  • Ratio: 4.1:1 imbalance

Features: 26 per foot
  • Arch Index (primary, 19% importance)
  • Contact areas by zone (forefoot, midfoot, hindfoot)
  • Pressure statistics
  • Foot dimensions
  • Bilateral asymmetry
